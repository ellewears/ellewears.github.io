Privacy Policy for the Pinterest publishing application
Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ellewears handles information when its application is used to create and publish Pinterest Pins from product information and affiliate links.

1. Information the Application may process

  • Pinterest account information made available through Pinterest OAuth.
  • Pinterest access and refresh tokens required to authenticate API requests.
  • Pinterest board information needed to select a destination board for Pins.
  • Product URLs supplied by the user, including affiliate product links.
  • Public product information retrieved from supplied product pages, such as product titles, descriptions, images, and URLs.
  • Content generated for Pins, such as titles, descriptions, keywords, images, and videos.
  • Local application logs and processing status needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the requested workflow, including generating content, preparing Pins, selecting Pinterest boards, authenticating with Pinterest, publishing Pins, maintaining processing status, preventing duplicate processing, and troubleshooting errors.

3. Pinterest authentication

The application uses Pinterest OAuth for authorization. The application does not request or store the user's Pinterest password. Pinterest credentials and tokens are used only for authorized API operations on the connected Pinterest account.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens, refresh tokens, application configuration, logs, and generated content may be stored locally on the user's computer, depending on the user's configuration and use of the application.

Users are responsible for protecting their computer, configuration files, API keys, and authentication credentials. Private API keys and Pinterest tokens should not be published or shared.

5. Product and affiliate links

The application may process product and affiliate URLs supplied by the user. Those URLs may point to third-party websites such as online marketplaces. Third-party websites have their own privacy policies and terms, which are not controlled by Ellewears.

6. Third-party services

The application may use third-party services configured by the user for functions such as AI content generation, image generation, video generation, product-page retrieval, and Pinterest API access. Information sent to such services is governed by the applicable service's terms and privacy policy.

7. Data sharing and sale

Ellewears does not sell personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to provide a service explicitly configured or requested by the user, such as Pinterest API access or an AI generation service.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because the application can operate locally, the user can remove locally stored application data, logs, generated files, tokens, and configuration files from their computer. Retention by third-party services is governed by those services' own policies.

9. Children's privacy

The application is not intended for children under 13, and the application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
